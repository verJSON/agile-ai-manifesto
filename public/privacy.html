Privacy

Last updated November 9, 2026.

Your choice

This website is operated by verJSON. Optional Microsoft Clarity analytics is off until you accept, wherever you visit from. Rejecting analytics does not limit access to the manifesto. You can change your choice using Privacy settings on every page.

Microsoft Clarity

If you accept, Microsoft Clarity processes page views, clicks, scrolling, device and browser information, and session replay to help us understand how visitors use this site and improve it. Session replay reconstructs interactions with page content. Microsoft provides and processes this analytics service. Clarity may set cookies to connect interactions across pages. We send advertising storage consent as denied; accepting analytics does not grant advertising consent.

Microsoft documents a 30-day retention period for ordinary playback data. Click and heatmap data, labeled or favorited recordings, and selected recordings may remain for up to 9 months. See Microsoft's retention documentation, its recordings overview, and the Microsoft Privacy Statement for service details.

Remembering and withdrawing your choice

We store your preference in this site's browser storage for no more than one year (the current policy may expire it sooner), solely to remember your choice. Clearing browser storage removes the preference. A material policy or provider change requires a new choice. We do not share your choice across unrelated domains.

Use Privacy settings and reject analytics to withdraw. When Clarity has already loaded, the page may reload to stop its running code. Withdrawal prevents future collection; it does not retroactively delete information already received by Microsoft. If configuration cannot be loaded, analytics stays off.

Essential delivery

Cloudflare delivers this website and processes network requests needed to serve it. The manifesto also loads fonts from Google Fonts. These delivery requests are separate from optional Clarity analytics.
